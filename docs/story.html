Why monaco-json-studio-single Exists

1-pane static Monaco JSON Studio generator composing base-header, base-body, and base-footer.

Design Story

In a 1-pane editor, stacking a separate pane header below the studio bar wastes vertical space.

monaco-json-studio-single (v2) directly composes monaco-json-studio-base-header, monaco-json-studio-base-body (single), and monaco-json-studio-base-footer through document.hbs into a clean, non-overwriting static studio folder.
